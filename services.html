What’s included?
  • 60 minutes working directly with me on agreed coursework or test-preparation goals.
  • Session preparation, including reviewing relevant materials shared beforehand.
  • A brief parent recap of what we worked on, what I noticed, and the next priority.
  • Suggested between-session practice when useful.

Unlimited homework checking, on-demand tutoring by text, and additional planning meetings are not included. If more support would help, we’ll agree on its scope and cost separately.

How do we get started?
  1. Talk first. A complimentary 30–45 minute parent conversation to understand your concerns and see whether I can help.
  2. Try a session. A 60-minute session at $150 to work on representative problems and identify useful priorities.
  3. Decide together. I’ll recommend whether ongoing tutoring makes sense and how often to meet. If we continue, we’ll review progress after about four sessions.

There is no required assessment fee or multi-session purchase to start.

How do scheduling and rates work?

We’ll confirm the schedule, location or online format, payment arrangements, and cancellation terms before the first session.

Your $150 session rate stays fixed for your first 12 weeks. Any later rate change comes with at least 30 days’ notice, and any longer agreed price commitment is honored.

More extensive support is agreed individually, with a clear scope, fee, and review date before it begins.
