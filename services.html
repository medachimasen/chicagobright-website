What’s included?
  • 60 minutes working directly with me on agreed coursework or test-preparation goals.
  • Session preparation, including reviewing relevant materials shared beforehand.
  • A brief parent recap of what we worked on, what I noticed, and the next priority.
  • Suggested between-session practice when useful.

Unlimited homework checking, on-demand tutoring by text, and additional planning meetings are not included. If more support would help, we’ll agree on its scope and cost separately.

How do we get started?
  1. Talk first. A complimentary 30–45 minute parent conversation to understand your concerns and see whether I can help.
  2. Try a session. A 60-minute session at $150 to work on representative problems and identify useful priorities.
  3. Decide together. I’ll recommend whether ongoing tutoring makes sense and how often to meet. If we continue, we’ll review progress after about four sessions.

There is no required assessment fee or multi-session purchase to start.

How do scheduling and rates work?

We’ll confirm the schedule, location or online format, payment arrangements, and cancellation terms before the first session.

Your $150 session rate stays fixed for your first 12 weeks. Any later rate change comes with at least 30 days’ notice, and any longer agreed price commitment is honored.

More extensive support is agreed individually, with a clear scope, fee, and review date before it begins.

How is the four-week ACT course scheduled?

The course format is either four weekly 4-hour weekend meetings or eight 2-hour weekday meetings, held twice a week. Both total 16 teaching hours over four weeks. Contact Ben to discuss the upcoming schedule.

Three additional 60-minute virtual group office-hour sessions are scheduled in weeks 2, 3, and 4. All three dates will be provided before enrollment. No office-hour session is included in week 1 or after the final scheduled support session.

How do ACT office hours work?

Use office hours for questions about current or earlier ACT lessons, assigned practice, missed questions, and course-related study strategies.

Sign up and submit one priority question or topic by 6 p.m. Central the day before the session, using the signup channel provided with registration. One signup is enough for the session to run. When it runs, all enrolled students may attend, including to listen.

If nobody signs up by the cutoff, the session is canceled and the group is notified. It does not carry forward or become an individual appointment credit. Late requests go to the next scheduled session, if one remains. If Ben cancels a requested session, a replacement group session of the same length will be arranged with notice.

The group shares 60 minutes. Similar questions are combined, and students with unaddressed questions get priority before repeat turns, with priority rotating across sessions. Individual tutoring time and answers to every question are not guaranteed. Unfinished topics may move to the next scheduled session or an existing textbook or practice reference.

The allowance ends with the final scheduled support session. It does not include replacement lessons for missed classes, full private tutoring sessions, unrelated subject tutoring, admissions counseling, new individual study plans, full practice-test grading, or unlimited email support. Additional private help can be discussed separately, subject to availability.
