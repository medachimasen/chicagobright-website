Privacy

Privacy policy.

How ChicagoBright handles information when you visit this website or contact Ben.

Effective October 30, 2026

Information you provide

This website does not create user accounts or collect information through an on-site form. If you email, call, or text ChicagoBright, you may choose to provide information such as your name, contact details, and information about the tutoring support you are seeking. That information is used to respond to you, discuss services, and maintain appropriate business records.

Website analytics

ChicagoBright uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website and improve its content. Google Analytics may collect page views, session statistics, approximate location, browser and device information, and a client identifier stored in a first-party cookie named _ga. ChicagoBright does not use this website to sell personal information.

Google processes Analytics data under its own policies. You can review Google’s Privacy Policy and use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. You can also restrict or delete cookies through your browser settings.

Scheduling through Calendly

If you select “Schedule a time,” you will leave this website and use Calendly. Information submitted on the Calendly booking page is processed through Calendly and used to arrange and manage the meeting. Please review Calendly’s Privacy Notice for details.

Children’s information

This website is intended for parents and guardians seeking tutoring services. ChicagoBright does not knowingly ask children to submit personal information through this website. Parents and guardians should make initial contact on a student’s behalf.

Retention and security

Direct correspondence is retained only as reasonably needed to respond, provide services, maintain business records, and meet applicable obligations. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website’s practices or services change. The effective date above will be revised when material updates are made.

Contact

For questions about this policy or your information, email ben@chicagobright.com or call or text (312) 469-0810.
